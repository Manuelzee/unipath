---
name: Subscription marketing and existing access
description: Plan positioning is not proof of feature availability or permission to remove working free tools.
---

Treat requested paid-plan benefits as desired positioning where the
implementation is absent, not as evidence that the capability exists.
Do not add paywalls to existing working tools merely because the same
capabilities also appear in a proposed paid tier. Preserve established
prices and existing features unless the user authorizes changing them.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires truthful plan descriptions, no
invented features, and no removal of existing features. Advanced
comparison, AI assistance, and human-support marketing must not imply
that services have been implemented or can already be purchased.

**How to apply:** Check actual capabilities before changing plan copy.
Distinguish available, source-limited, and planned benefits. Clearly
explain unavailable payment or fulfillment setup instead of simulating
checkout, subscriptions, bookings, or receipts. Do not promise application
submission, admissions, or scholarship outcomes without explicit
implementation and authorization.

Subscription/service prices are distinct from institution tuition/cost
figures; the student-facing school-price restriction does not hide
UniPath's own plan prices.
