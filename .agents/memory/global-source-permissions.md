The user wants Canada first, tested before later country phases, while retaining
the U.S. directory, existing design and subscription plans. Never call a country
fully covered merely because one source has been imported.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested phased international expansion and honest
coverage, and is a beginner who expects technical setup to be handled.

**How to apply:** Review each source's reuse rights and actual institution/program
coverage before importing. Verify real database records before reporting success.

WHED, CICIC and EduCanada access does not automatically permit bulk commercial
redistribution. Terms checked during this expansion require IAU consent for
substantial WHED copying, prohibit CICIC automated extraction/significant storage,
and require EduCanada commercial-redistribution permission unless separately licensed.

**Why:** UniPath AI has subscription plans; public website access is not a reuse licence.

**How to apply:** Recheck current terms and obtain applicable written permission
or an expressly permitted export/API before adding those datasets. Use reference
links rather than scraping restricted directories.

Government PSC co-op/internship validation is not general recognition/accreditation,
current operation, current program availability, or IRCC DLI status. English/French
source labels do not establish language of instruction.

**Why:** Source semantics differ; a government-hosted list can include historical
names and cannot substantiate unrelated institution or applicant-eligibility claims.

**How to apply:** Mark unsourced facts unknown, distinguish import/source dates from
independent verification, and never infer a school website from its name/contact
email or international eligibility from a co-op listing. Link official lookups
without presenting them as verification of the record.

Government institution-level tuition must not automatically be assigned to a
campus-only directory entry. Leave ambiguous matches unknown unless an official
source explicitly supports the campus and scope.

**Why:** B.C.'s open arts-tuition data identify whole institutions, while parts
of the Canadian directory identify individual campuses. Similar names alone
do not establish that a campus offers the program or charges that amount.

**How to apply:** Use exact unambiguous identities, keep program-specific scope
visible, and obtain source-backed campus mappings before extending matches.
