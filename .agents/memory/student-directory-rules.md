---
name: Student directory rules
description: Product rules for UniPath AI's student-facing college directory and official information.
---

The user now requests student-facing annual tuition comparisons, budget
filters, preferred-currency estimates and optional total annual cost estimates.
Keep the existing UniPath AI design and subscription prices.

**Why:** The explicit Annual Tuition Budget & Global Payments requirements
uploaded on 2026-11-10 supersede the earlier ban on school tuition displays.

**How to apply:** Display only source-backed figures with their period, source,
reporting date and domestic/international category. Currency conversions must
have verified rates and timestamps. Mark missing or outdated data explicitly;
do not invent worldwide tuition coverage or guaranteed scholarship funding.
Preserve skippable private student budget inputs. Missing costs,
program intakes, admission conditions and award eligibility remain unknown;
an application priority date is not a final deadline or proof that admissions
have closed. A preference score is not an admission or funding probability.

Treat saved budget limits and personal living-expense estimates as private
planning information, not customer revenue or an administrator's financial report.

**Why:** The student budget UI promises privacy. A request for customer-management
layouts does not authorize exposing students' own financial limits.

**How to apply:** Customer management can show account references and profile
coverage while financial reporting uses actual provider records only. Never
turn a student's budget maximum into tuition data or projected revenue.

Use official sources and identify the source and last-updated date. Never invent missing information. Link to official school admissions and financial aid pages when supplied; otherwise clearly label the official-homepage fallback.

**Why:** Scorecard does not contain complete current admissions requirements or scholarship opportunities, and the user requires honest coverage.

**How to apply:** Do not claim data has been imported until the database is working correctly. Distinguish source reporting dates, directory import dates, and current verification. The owner is a beginner; handle setup where possible and give exact file/upload instructions.
