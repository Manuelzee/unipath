### 1. Annual Tuition Budget
Add a field called **“Annual Tuition Budget”** to help students find schools within their budget.

Allow students to select:
- Under $5,000
- $5,000–$10,000
- $10,000–$20,000
- $20,000–$35,000
- $35,000–$50,000
- $50,000+
- I need scholarships or financial aid
- No specific budget
- Enter my own budget

Requirements:
- Let students select their preferred currency.
- Allow students to indicate whether their budget is for tuition only or their total annual education budget.
- For international schools, account for currency differences.
- Use verified tuition information for matching when available.
- Consider scholarships and financial aid when evaluating affordability, but never guarantee that a student will receive funding.
- If tuition data is missing or outdated, clearly label it as unverified.
- **Important:** Do not display annual tuition or annual cost figures on student-facing university profiles. Use this information internally for matching and show students affordability indicators, such as “May fit your budget,” “Scholarship opportunities available,” or “Budget information needs verification.”

### 2. Start Term

Allow students to select:
- Spring
- Summer
- Fall
- Winter
- Flexible / Any term

Add a separate field for the preferred start year, with options for the current year and future years.

Requirements:
- Match students with institutions and programs that accept applications for their selected start term.
- Check official university admissions pages for term availability and deadlines.
- Clearly identify deadlines that have passed.

- If information is unavailable, display “Start term availability needs verification.”
