# UniPath AI — Annual Tuition Budget & Global Payments

Upgrade my existing UniPath AI app and website with two new features: **Annual Tuition Budget** and **Worldwide Payments with Multiple Currencies and Cryptocurrency**.

## 1. Annual Tuition Budget

Add a required or skippable "Annual Tuition Budget" section to the student onboarding profile and university search filters.

Allow students to:
- Enter their maximum annual tuition budget.
- Select their preferred currency, including USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD, NGN, INR, CNY, AED, and other supported currencies.
- Choose a budget range or enter a custom amount.
- Filter universities worldwide based on annual tuition costs.
- Compare tuition fees across universities.
- View estimated tuition in their preferred currency.
- Find scholarships and financial aid that may reduce tuition costs.
- Compare domestic and international tuition rates where applicable.
- See whether tuition estimates are per year, semester, or program.
- View the tuition data source and last updated date.

Show an optional estimated total annual cost including tuition, accommodation, food, transportation, and other expenses.

Do not treat estimated scholarship awards as guaranteed funding. Clearly identify missing or outdated tuition information.

## 2. Worldwide Customer Payments

Build a secure payment system that allows customers worldwide to purchase UniPath AI subscriptions and application support services.

Support these existing plans:
- Free — $0
- Student Plus — $9.99/month
- Premium — $29.99/month
- Application Support — $99–$299 per package

Use USD as the initial base pricing currency and allow customers to view prices in supported local currencies.

Integrate a reputable payment provider such as Stripe for card payments and supported local payment methods.

Allow customers to:
- Select their preferred currency.
- See the exact amount payable before checkout.
- Pay with supported credit/debit cards and local payment methods.
- Receive payment confirmations and receipts.
- Manage subscriptions.
- Cancel recurring subscriptions.
- View payment history and invoices.

Display currency conversion rates, any applicable fees, and final checkout amounts accurately. Do not assume every payment method or currency is available in every country.

## 3. Cryptocurrency Payments

Add an optional "Pay with Crypto" payment method through a compliant cryptocurrency payment processor.

Support cryptocurrency options such as:
- Bitcoin (BTC)
- Ethereum (ETH)
- USDC
- USDT, where supported by the selected provider

Only enable currencies, tokens, and blockchain networks actually supported by the payment processor and legally available to the merchant and customer.

Use provider-hosted checkout rather than collecting private wallet keys.

Show:
- Cryptocurrency and network
- Exact payment amount
- Network fees when available
- Payment expiration time
- Pending, confirmed, failed, or expired status
- Transaction reference and receipt

Do not mark a cryptocurrency payment as successful until the provider verifies final settlement.

For recurring subscriptions, use a provider-supported recurring payment method. Do not assume cryptocurrency payments support automatic renewals.

## 4. Admin Payment Dashboard

Create a secure admin dashboard where I can:
- View customer purchases and subscriptions.
- See payment amounts and currencies.
- Track completed, pending, refunded, and failed transactions.
- View revenue reports.
- Manage refunds and subscription status.
- Export payment records.
- Configure supported currencies and payment methods.

Restrict financial and customer data to authorized administrators.

## 5. Security and Compliance

- Keep all payment API credentials in server-side environment variables, never in code.
- Never store raw card numbers, CVVs, private keys, or seed phrases.
- Use secure hosted checkout.
- Verify payment provider webhooks.
- Prevent duplicate payments and duplicate subscription activation.
- Support applicable tax, refund, and consumer disclosure requirements.
- Use verified exchange rates and clearly display conversion timestamps.
- Follow provider policies and applicable cryptocurrency regulations.
- Use sandbox/test environments before enabling live payments.

## 6. Design

Match the existing UniPath AI branding and mobile-friendly layout.

Add:
- Annual Tuition Budget to student onboarding.
- Tuition Budget filters to university search.
- Billing & Payments to student account settings.
- Currency selector to the pricing page.
- Pay with Crypto only when the selected provider is configured and supported.
- Revenue & Transactions to the administrator dashboard.

Build and test these features without disrupting university search, applications, scholarships, or Global Student Jobs.

**Do not publish or enable live payments until I approve.**

Report which features are complete, which payment providers require account setup, and which API secrets are needed.
