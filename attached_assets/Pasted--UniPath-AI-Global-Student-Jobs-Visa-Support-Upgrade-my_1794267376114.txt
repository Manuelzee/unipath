# UniPath AI — Global Student Jobs & Visa Support

Upgrade my existing UniPath AI app and website by adding a complete worldwide job search platform for international students studying on student visas.

**Main objective:** Help international students discover real employment opportunities, understand applicable work restrictions, and prepare job applications.

## 1. Worldwide job search

Support countries worldwide, initially prioritizing the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Germany, France, Ireland, New Zealand, Netherlands, Japan, Singapore, and UAE.

Allow students to filter jobs by:
- Country, city, and university
- Student visa category
- Work authorization status
- On-campus or off-campus location
- Part-time, internship, seasonal, or graduate employment
- Job category and industry
- Hourly pay or salary
- Required skills and experience
- Employer sponsorship availability
- Remote, hybrid, or on-site work

## 3. Live job listings

Integrate legitimate job listing APIs and official university career portals where access and licensing permit.

Every listing must show:
- Job title and employer
- Country and city
- Salary or hourly pay when available
- Work schedule
- Job description and requirements
- Application deadline when available
- Posting date and source
- Employer application link
- Visa sponsorship information when verified
- Work authorization requirements
- Date the listing was last checked

Never invent job openings. Clearly label expired, unverified, or unavailable listings.

## 4. AI student visa work eligibility

Create a country-specific AI assistant that helps students understand employment restrictions.

Ask students for their destination country, visa type, study status, work authorization, and university before providing personalized guidance.

Support country-specific pathways, including US F-1/J-1 and CPT/OPT, Canadian study permit conditions, UK Student visa rules, and equivalent rules elsewhere.

Use official government immigration guidance and record the source and verification date.

Classify listings as:
- Potentially eligible — authorization still needs verification
- Additional authorization required
- Likely restricted
- Eligibility unknown

Never guarantee that a job is legal for a particular student. Explain when university or immigration authority confirmation is required.

## 6. Student dashboard

Add a navigation tab called **Global Student Jobs**.

The dashboard should include:
- Recommended jobs
- On-campus opportunities
- Internships
- Part-time jobs
- Graduate opportunities
- Visa and work eligibility guidance
- Saved jobs
- My job applications

Reuse the student's existing UniPath AI profile and education preferences, with permission.

## 7. Design and reliability

Use the existing UniPath AI branding with a modern, premium, mobile-friendly interface.

Support local currencies, languages, time zones, and accessible navigation.

Include scam-reporting features, privacy protections, job listing moderation, and secure handling of student data.

Make the job directory worldwide and scalable. Do not display fake jobs as live opportunities.

**Final goal:** UniPath AI should become an all-in-one international student platform for university discovery, scholarships, admission preparation, visa guidance, and employment discovery worldwide.
